Let's build a simple contacts app. Every contact needs a full name, a
phone number, an email address, and a category — like family, friend, or
work. I want to add a contact, edit their details later, and delete
someone if I need to. There should also be a way to mark a contact as a
favorite, and I want to filter the list down to just favorites, or just
one category at a time. One screen, nothing fancy — a form to add people
and a list underneath showing everyone.
